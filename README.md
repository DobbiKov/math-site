# Yehor's Math & CS Notes

Welcome! This is a growing collection of **notes, cheatsheets, worked proofs and — above all — carefully curated learning resources** in mathematics and computer science. Everything here is what I actually used and liked while studying, gathered in one place and shared freely.

Access the live site: [dobbikov.github.io/math-site](https://dobbikov.github.io/math-site/)

## Who this is for

- **Anyone looking for good math/CS resources.** For each topic you'll find hand-picked YouTube channels, textbooks, lecture notes and short explainer videos — with a note on *why* each one is worth your time.
- **Students of the LDD Maths-Info (Paris-Saclay).** The material follows that curriculum closely, so you can use it as a companion through your three years. See the [LDD Maths-Info path](#ldd-maths-info-path) below.

## How to find things

Use the sidebar (left). Pages are grouped **by subject area** so you can jump straight to what you need:

- **[General resources](./resources.md)** — my favourite channels and cross-cutting recommendations. *Start here if you just want great teachers to follow.*
- **First-Year Foundations** — where to begin if you're new to university maths.
- **Analysis & Calculus** — analysis, measure theory & integration, differential equations, topology.
- **Algebra & Linear Algebra** — abstract algebra, linear algebra, computational (numerical) linear algebra.
- **Probability & Statistics** — probability theory, statistical inference, and my worked notes/exercises.
- **Optimisation & Differential Calculus** — convex optimisation, KKT, duality.
- **Computer Science** — logic, compilation, distributed algorithms, machine learning (statistical learning).
- **Electives** — other courses (e.g. Droit / Law).

Most pages are **resource lists**; some also contain my own worked notes and proofs (e.g. measure theory), and a few include LaTeX write-ups, exercise sheets (TDs) and problem sets (DMs).
