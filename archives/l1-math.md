# L1 Mathematics

I recommend just one channel - [3blue1brown](https://www.youtube.com/c/3blue1brown). He provides two playlists:
- [analysis](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZHQObOWTQDMsr9K-rj53DwVRMYO3t5Yr&si=MW-hY1LwTZHQR0CH)
- [linear algebra](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZHQObOWTQDPD3MizzM2xVFitgF8hE_ab)

Both are availible in Englis, French and even more. Even though he provides a
solid understanding and intuition for both subjects, in my opinion, he does
even more important work: he gives you the love to math, forces you to build
the theory yourself, to see the joy. I can't say more, just watch it please.
